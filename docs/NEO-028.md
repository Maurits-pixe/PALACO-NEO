# NEO-028 — Dual-signed quorum policy succession (DRAFT)

## Implemented
- `keyQuorumPolicyDigest` from NEO-027 remains the canonical input for each versioned policy.
- `policySuccessionPayload` binds each reviewer signature to the exact old/new policy digests, namespace, versions, chain position, predecessor hash, decision ID, reviewer identity and role. Only pre-change ACTIVE reviewer keys may sign.
- `verifyPolicySuccessionChain` replays an append-only, SHA-256-linked policy history from an externally authenticated initial policy pin; each event requires distinct Ed25519 OWNER + SECURITY signatures under the **previous** policy.
- At most one auditable reviewer addition, revocation or replacement key rotation is accepted per event. Existing reviewer roles cannot change, revoked reviewer identities cannot return, and a successor must maintain an active OWNER and SECURITY. The required two-role quorum is fixed in the NEO-028 prototype, not user-configurable.
- Duplicate decisions, malformed policies, forged signatures, invalid policy succession and rollback beneath an externally pinned chain count/head fail closed.
- Fourteen regression tests cover succession, retroactive self-approval, compromised quorum, role reassignment, mutation, stale pins and rollback.

## Security limits
These functions do **not** sign, publish, persist or activate policies and do not grant authority. No new policy is automatically adopted on success; the trusted custodian must separately verify and pin the successor under a controlled process.

This draft is not a generalized N-of-M quorum engine. It does not provide time-of-signature proofs, true historical reviewer revocation timing, hardware-backed reviewer identities, independent durable storage, a cross-repo transaction, human operational approval or authentication of the original trust anchors. An attacker who controls the external initial policy pin and ledger floor could still rewrite the apparent history. Full chain signatures plus independent pins are prerequisites, not independent custody by themselves.

GO-13 HOLD; `authorized:false` always. No merge to main, intentional production deployment, key activation or external writes.
