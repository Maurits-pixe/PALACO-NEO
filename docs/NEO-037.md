# NEO-037 — Dual-reviewed recovery evidence and post-repair inventory (DRAFT)

## Implemented
- `recoveryProposalDigest` canonicalizes and SHA-256 hashes a NEO-036 repair proposal, including the pinned case, inventory, custody-lineage count/head, affected replicas and missing artifact IDs. Rejects duplicate or malformed IDs.
- `recoveryReviewPayload` binds separate Ed25519 OWNER and SECURITY statements to namespace, unique recovery ID, pinned review-policy digest, repair proposal digest, inventory hash and custody-lineage position.
- `verifyRecoveryEvidence` reruns the entire NEO-036 pre-repair assessment, requiring a legitimate REPAIR_PROPOSAL_ONLY state with at least two complete replica byte inventories. It rejects revoked/forged/duplicate reviewers and wrong policy pins.
- All post-repair replica keys and expected IDs must match the original roster; their observation IDs must be new. All original artifact bytes must be present and SHA-256 correct in **every** post-repair replica. Otherwise HOLD. Successful assessment is labeled POST_REPAIR_EVIDENCE_VERIFIED_NO_AUTHORITY and never authorizes repair or release.
- Seventeen regression tests cover valid evidence, missing or changed bytes, stale observations, swapped replica keys, forged reviewers, incorrect policy pins and malformed proposals.

## Limitations
This module is a **read-only verifier**. It does NOT perform the repair, approve execution, access live storage, sign review messages, persist a replay register or establish a trustworthy post-write observation. Its pre/post snapshots and independent trust pins must be gathered through a secure external channel. Fresh observation IDs are checked against supplied pre-state, but persistent non-replay and real-world freshness are not established. Two cryptographically different reviewer keys do not prove legal independence or human approval.

A signature on a recovery proposal does not override GO-13 or authorize execution; no file is overwritten and no escalation case is closed. Independently observed durable storage, authenticated signed post-repair checkpoints, immutable provenance and a separate operational authorization gate remain essential before production.

GO-13 HOLD / DENY. No merge, deployment, activation or external write. Every result carries `authorized:false`.
