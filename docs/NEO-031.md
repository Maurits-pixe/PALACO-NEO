# NEO-031 — Multi-witness checkpoint recovery (DRAFT)

## Implemented
- `reconcilePolicyWitnesses`: validates the full NEO-030 activation ledger and **each original signed checkpoint** against externally supplied Ed25519 witness keys and checkpoint floors. Requires 2–16 independently named and differently keyed witnesses, exact expected IDs, no missing observations and matching chain head/count and policy digest. A pending external policy that still matches the predecessor remains HOLD (no silent retry).
- `classifyPolicyCheckpointRecovery`: read-only comparison of trusted and observed checkpoint positions against supplied entry hashes. Distinguishes UNCHANGED, PENDING_INDEPENDENT_REVIEW, rollback and forks. **It does not authenticate its supplied entries**, so never use it alone to trust a chain.
- Sixteen regression tests for signatures, witness identity/key duplication, revoked witnesses, forked/stale observation, rollback, externally lagging state and malformed inputs.

## Explicit limitations
Two cryptographically distinct keys do not prove that the custodians are physically, legally, operationally or administratively independent. The exact expected witness roster, public keys, key status, latest trusted checkpoint floors, initial policy pin, and full historical custodian trust must be obtained through independent authenticated custody.

No actual network witness, durable storage, distributed consensus, freshness proof, trusted clock, automatic crash recovery, checkpoint publishing or policy activation is implemented. A valid signed checkpoint is evidence of a witness assertion, not proof of an atomic external commit. A more sophisticated externally trusted wall-clock and anti-equivocation mechanism are needed before production.

Every classification has `authorized:false`. GO-13 HOLD / DENY. No merge, deployment, issuance or external writes.
