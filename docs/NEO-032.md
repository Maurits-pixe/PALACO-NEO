# NEO-032 — Signed witness equivocation dossiers and escalation (DRAFT)

## Implemented
- `inspectWitnessEquivocation(first,second,trust)`: verifies two **original Ed25519 checkpoint signatures** (NEO-030 payload) against the same separately trusted witness ID, namespace, public key and checkpoint floor. Two different signed claims at the **same count** constitute evidence of signing-key equivocation. Different counts can be legitimate progress and are not treated as proof of equivocation. Identical claims are not a conflict.
- A deterministic SHA-256 dossier records both full signed seals, namespace, signer, count and a canonical order-independent digest.
- `verifyWitnessEquivocationDossier`: independently re-verifies both original signatures and the dossier checksum, instead of trusting an unsigned classifier assertion.
- `classifyWitnessEscalation`: produces ESCALATE_HOLD when genuine conflicting signed claims are present. Informal OWNER/SECURITY review flags **cannot** release HOLD or revoke a signer.
- Fifteen regression tests exercise conflicting checkpoints and policy digests, reordering, forgery, revoked witnesses, invalid floors, modified dossiers, and fail-closed escalation.

## Strict operational boundary
The witness public key, namespace, key validity and trusted count/head floor must be authenticated from external custody. A valid pair of signatures proves contradictory statements by one signing key under a pinned trust model, **not** the identity or intent of the key holder, physical independence, actual durability, freshness or legal misconduct. A floor pin that already commits to one side may cause the other to be rejected instead of treated as admissible historical evidence; forensic investigation requires independently retained historical trust snapshots.

There is no automatic revocation, quarantine, publication, alarm delivery, consensus, database write or case management. The review flags are illustrative untrusted input, not signed governance acts. None of the functions grant authority (`authorized:false`). GO-13 HOLD/DENY; no merge or production deployment.
