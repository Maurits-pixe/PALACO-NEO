Emits `RECEIPT_TRANSFER_INCIDENT_CHECKPOINT_CONFLICT_HOLD` and a stable evidence fingerprint; it neither chooses a true branch nor grants authority.

## Security limits
NEO-065 is read-only and does not create signatures, persist a secure audit database, operate a remote archive, close incidents, notify people or grant privileges. Separate Ed25519 keys do not establish legal, physical or organizational independence. Effective erasure protection requires independently authenticated and monotonically retained checkpoint floor pins and trustworthy full original histories; a genesis pin cannot detect wholesale deletion of an unpinned history.
