# NEO-046 — Emergency quarantine of active custodian keys (DRAFT)

## Implementation
- `emergencyQuarantinePayload`: domain-separated Ed25519 signature payload binding namespace, sequence, predecessor emergency-register head, unique incident ID, **currently active** custodian/key fingerprint, independent successor identity and public-key fingerprint, complete NEO-044 lifecycle digest and audit history position, and pinned NEO-027 review-policy digest. Only `QUARANTINE_ACTIVE_KEY_HOLD` is permitted.
- `verifyEmergencyQuarantineJournal`: revalidates the full original NEO-044 key lifecycle and underlying NEO-043/042/041/040 evidence, authenticates the pinned policy, verifies independent OWNER and SECURITY signatures and a signed SUCCESSOR key-possession statement, and checks hash-chain integrity and externally retained floor. Refuses a target not active at the pinned snapshot, an already active successor identity, any reused/retired/current/reviewer successor key, duplicate incidents or repeated quarantine of the same active key, and tampered or truncated history.
- `emergencyQuarantineEntryHash`: SHA-256 commitment to signed reviewer claims and successor acknowledgement.
- Twenty-four regression tests cover successful verification without authority, pinning, revoked signers, forged signatures, active/retired key misuse, forbidden activation, duplicate incidents, corruption, rollback and fork.

## Operational boundary
**This is an evidence-only emergency proposal, not a live revocation or replacement.** It never removes a key from a running system, grants a successor access, issues credentials, modifies trust registries or installs a key. The successor's signature proves possession of its private key, not entitlement to act as custodian. A real emergency response needs a separately approved, atomic, auditable deny gate and securely managed trust-rotation process; it must not depend solely on caller-supplied observations.

The journal is snapshot-scoped: every entry binds to one verified NEO-044 lifecycle digest and an independently pinned policy. A new lifecycle needs a separate migration mechanism to carry forward old quarantine records. Externally retained monotonically advancing `{count,head}` floors are necessary to detect erasure. Reviewer keys, the caller-supplied history and offline possession proofs cannot establish trusted time, human identity or actual remote storage state.

Every result is `authorized:false`. GO-13 HOLD/DENY; no merge, release, automatic service revocation, installation, deployment, or external write.
