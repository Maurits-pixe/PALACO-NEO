# NEO-020 — Recovery trust-anchor transitions and reconciliation (DRAFT)

## Implemented
- `proposeRecoveryAnchor` validates a before-register against a separately pinned anchor (namespace, revision, SHA-256 digest) and confirms an exact ISSUED→CONSUMED transition for one challenge with no other record changes.
- `reconcileRecoveryAnchor` classifies current observed register as NOT_COMMITTED, COMMITTED or HOLD against a previously retained before/proposed anchor pair.
- `verifyAnchorSequence` checks revision monotonicity and computes a deterministic sequence checksum, without claiming authentication.
- Ten tests cover rollback, invalid transitions, conflicting records and revision overflow.

## Critical limits
This module **does not** provide independent anchor custody or a transaction spanning challenge-file and external anchor updates. It produces read-only proposals and observations. The calling authority must safely, atomically and independently persist accepted anchors, establish their authenticity, and reconcile uncertain commits before retry. The sequence checksum is NOT an attestation. A digest mismatch is HOLD, not a license to overwrite either state.

No background service, production deployment, key grant, authorization or merge. GO-13 HOLD / DENY remain active.
