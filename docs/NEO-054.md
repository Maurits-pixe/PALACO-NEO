# NEO-054 — Receipt-checkpoint equivocation incident register (DRAFT)

## Implemented
- `receiptIncidentReviewPayload`: domain-separated Ed25519 OWNER and SECURITY review statement bound to namespace, unique incident ID, checkpoint identity/height, canonical checkpoint SHA-256 hash, conflicting custodian ID, re-derived NEO-053 equivocation digest, pinned NEO-027 review policy digest, previous incident register hash and **only** allowable action `ESCALATE_RECEIPT_EQUIVOCATION_HOLD`.
- `receiptIncidentEntryHash`: deterministic SHA-256 commitment to the entire incident scope and both named reviewer signatures.
- `verifyReceiptIncidentJournal`: independently replays the **entire original NEO-053 canonical receipt checkpoint history and all NEO-052/051/050/... archival evidence** for every incident; rejects changed historical bytes, revoked checkpoint signers, invalid NEO-051 receipts and substituted histories. It checks that the claimed canonical statement and trusted custodian public key exactly match a verified original NEO-053 checkpoint record at the required sequence and predecessor. The alternative witness claim is independently verified by `inspectReceiptCheckpointEquivocation`, and matching statements are not accepted as conflict evidence.
- Requires two different ACTIVE reviewer identities and keys covering OWNER and SECURITY with valid Ed25519 signatures on the full incident. Forbids duplicate incident IDs and replayed equivocation fingerprints in the supplied log; refuses unauthorized closure actions, forged signatures, malformed scope, altered SHA-256 entry hashes, and truncation/forks relative to an independently retained monotonic incident floor.
- Twenty-one regressions cover real signed conflict evidence, canonical source and archival byte tampering, forged and matching counterclaims, reviewer scope changes, duplicate signatures and IDs, revocation, rollback, independent pins and forbidden closure.

## Explicit limitations
All output is `authorized:false` and conflict status is `ESCALATED_HOLD`. This is cryptographic evidence **only**; it never sends an operational notification to OWNER/SECURITY, identifies a guilty individual, closes a case, grants access, modifies a live trust registry, creates an immutable database or determines which conflicting historical statement is true. An empty register at the genesis floor cannot prove no historic cases exist. An independent authenticated and monotonically retained `{count,head}` register floor is essential to resist erasure or full history replacement.

Only signatures from a cryptographic key are verified; the physical identity and organizational independence of the reviewers still require out-of-band controls. Complete original evidence, trust roots, and external pins remain responsibilities of the calling system.
